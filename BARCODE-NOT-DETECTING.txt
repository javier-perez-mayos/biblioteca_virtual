❌ Any red error messages

📋 MOST COMMON ISSUES:

1. BARCODE NOT IN VIEW
   ✅ Center it in the GREEN rectangle
   ✅ Hold 10-15cm from camera
   ✅ Fill the frame but don't cut it off

2. POOR LIGHTING
   ✅ Use BRIGHT lighting
   ✅ No shadows on barcode
   ✅ No glare/reflections
   ✅ Turn on flash (mobile)

3. WRONG BARCODE TYPE
   ✅ Must be ISBN barcode
   ✅ Starts with 978 or 979
   ✅ NOT QR codes
   ✅ NOT other product barcodes

4. CAMERA NOT FOCUSED
   ✅ Wait 2-3 seconds for autofocus
   ✅ Clean camera lens
   ✅ Try moving closer/farther
   ✅ Tap screen to force focus (mobile)

5. BARCODE QUALITY
   ✅ Must be clean and clear
   ✅ Not damaged or scratched
   ✅ Not wrinkled or curved
   ✅ Printed properly

→ Not a detection problem

📊 WHAT BARCODES WORK:

✅ ISBN-13 (EAN-13) - Starts with 978/979
✅ ISBN-10 converted to EAN-13
✅ UPC-A codes
✅ EAN-8 codes

❌ QR codes - Not supported
❌ Data Matrix - Not supported
❌ Regular product barcodes - May not work

🎯 OPTIMAL CONDITIONS:

Distance:  10-15cm from camera
Lighting:  Bright, even, no shadows
Position:  Centered in green box
Angle:     Parallel to camera (not tilted)
Patience:  Hold steady for 2-3 seconds
Focus:     Wait for autofocus to work
